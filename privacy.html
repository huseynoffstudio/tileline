Privacy Policy for TileLine

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

Studio Huseynoff ("we", "our", or "us") operates the TileLine mobile application (the "Game"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Game and the choices you have associated with that data.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your gaming experience is safe and enjoyable.

1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Game to you.

A. Locally Stored Data (Game Preferences & Progress)

To provide core gameplay functionality (such as saving your current level, remaining hints, timers, and audio/vibration settings), TileLine stores game data locally on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore Preferences.

  • This data includes:
    • Current game level
    • Remaining hints and timer state
    • Sound and vibration preferences
  • We do not transmit, store, or access this local data on any external servers. All game progress and settings remain strictly on your device.

B. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob Ads)

TileLine integrates Google AdMob to display interstitial advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process data in accordance with its own privacy policies.

  • Data collected by Google AdMob may include:
    • Device identifiers (such as Advertising ID)
    • IP address and general network location information
    • Device and software characteristics (e.g., device model, operating system version)
    • Ad interaction data (e.g., ads viewed or clicked)
  • This information is used by Google and its partners to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud.
  • You can learn more about how Google uses data when you use our app by visiting Google's Privacy & Terms.

2. Use of Data

We use the collected and stored data for the following purposes:

  • To maintain and provide core game features (saving progress and preferences).
  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob to support the free operation of the Game.
  • To monitor and analyze ad performance and technical stability.

3. Children's Privacy

Our Game does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.

4. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us. Since your game progress is stored locally on your device, it is protected by your device's built-in security measures. However, remember that no method of electronic transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
